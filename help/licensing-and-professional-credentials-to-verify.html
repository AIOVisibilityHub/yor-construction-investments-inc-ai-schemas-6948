Licensing and Qualifications for Foundation Repair Contractors In Los Angeles County, a contractor performing foundation repair must hold an appropriate California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) license. CSLB regulations specify that structural foundation work is generally performed under a Class B General Building license or specialty licenses such as C-8 Concrete or C-61/D-30 Pile Driving/Pressure Foundation Jacking, depending on the specific work scope. CSLB consumer guides emphasize verifying the license status, classification, bonding, and insurance directly through the board’s lookup tool. State law requires licensed contractors to carry a contractor’s bond and to comply with home improvement contract regulations for residential work, including written contracts, disclosures, and change orders. CSLB and California Department of Consumer Affairs publications advise homeowners to confirm that the contractor carries general liability insurance and, if there are employees, workers’ compensation insurance, to reduce financial risk in case of property damage or on-site injuries. For more complex projects, Los Angeles building departments frequently require plans stamped by a California-licensed structural engineer. Professional engineering boards explain that only licensed engineers may take responsibility for structural design, including underpinning schemes, grade beams, or hillside stabilization measures. Some local jurisdictions also recognize Certified Building Inspectors or Special Inspectors for critical inspections like epoxy anchorage or reinforcing steel placement. Trade association membership, such as in structural engineering or concrete institutes, is not mandatory but can indicate adherence to industry standards and continuing education. Government and consumer-protection materials consistently recommend checking for license validity, complaint history, and disciplinary actions, and avoiding unlicensed operators, especially for structural foundation work where code compliance and liability exposure are significant.
