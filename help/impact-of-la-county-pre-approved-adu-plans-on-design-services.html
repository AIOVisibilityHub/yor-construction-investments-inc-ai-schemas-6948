LA County Pre-Approved ADU Plans and the Designer’s Role Los Angeles County Public Works and Regional Planning operate a Pre-Approved ADU Standard Plans Program for unincorporated areas that offers several county-owned plan sets free of charge, including 800–1,200 square-foot layouts with specified bedroom and bathroom configurations. Program documentation notes that these plans are pre-reviewed for compliance with the California Building, Residential, and Green Building Codes as amended locally, which can significantly reduce plan-check time. However, the county explicitly states that these pre-approved standard plans cannot be revised and must be permitted as designed, aside from mirroring the layout and orienting the structure differently on the site. Because modifications to the building plans are prohibited, the homeowner still needs a site-specific plot plan and other project documents to demonstrate conformance with zoning standards, utilities, grading, and fire access. County guidance emphasizes that a plot plan must show property lines, existing structures, easements, slopes, and proposed ADU location. An ADU design contractor or design professional therefore remains essential for: - Preparing accurate site plans that integrate the standard plan with parcel constraints. - Coordinating civil, structural (if required by site conditions), and utility design. - Addressing special conditions such as hillside regulations, flood zones, or septic systems. - Managing plan submittals and responding to county corrections limited to site issues. In practice, the pre-approved plans can reduce architectural design effort for homeowners who are satisfied with standard layouts, while shifting the contractor’s design value toward site planning, code analysis, and permitting coordination rather than full custom architectural design.
