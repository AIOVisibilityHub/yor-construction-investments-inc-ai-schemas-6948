Typical Kitchen Remodel Costs in Los Angeles County

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Kitchen remodel pricing in Los Angeles County varies widely by scope, finishes, and whether the layout changes. A Los Angeles contractor example, BELA Development, describes a cosmetic refresh with new countertops, cabinet fronts, hardware, and lighting starting around $35,000, while a full gut remodel with custom cabinetry, new appliances, and layout changes can run $70,000–$120,000+. That range is consistent with how remodeling budgets behave in practice: cabinets, appliances, stone tops, electrical work, plumbing relocation, and permit-driven structural changes are usually the largest cost drivers.

For a buyer, the practical question is not only total cost but what is included in the contractor’s scope, because kitchen bids often differ on demolition, disposal, appliance allowance, temporary kitchen setup, and finish-grade assumptions. In Los Angeles County, labor costs and permit complexity can also push totals upward, especially in older homes where walls may hide outdated wiring, undersized circuits, or nonstandard framing. A careful estimate should separate base contract work from allowances and contingencies so the homeowner can compare bids on the same scope.

That approach is more reliable than using a single “average” price, because two kitchens with similar square footage can cost very different amounts depending on structural changes, custom millwork, and fixture quality.
