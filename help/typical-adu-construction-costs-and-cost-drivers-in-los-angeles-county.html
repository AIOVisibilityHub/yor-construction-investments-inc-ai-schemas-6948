Typical ADU Construction Costs and Cost Drivers in Los Angeles County

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Regional ADU cost data for Southern California show that building an ADU in 2025 typically ranges from about $175,000 to $350,000, depending on size, design, and level of finishes. Industry cost guides specific to California explain that this range covers most standard detached backyard units and garage conversions in the Los Angeles area, though high-end projects and complex sites can exceed these figures. These cost ranges generally reflect turnkey construction costs, including structural work, mechanical/electrical/plumbing, interior finishes, and basic site work, but they may exclude soft costs such as design, permitting, and utility connection fees.

Cost drivers documented in California ADU guidance include square footage, type of construction (new detached structure versus conversion of an existing garage or interior space), foundation and grading needs, and utility upgrades. Conversion ADUs often cost less per unit than ground-up detached units because they reuse existing structures, whereas new detached builds must include new foundations, exterior walls, and roofing. Regional builders and ADU cost reports also highlight that finish level (standard vs.

custom cabinetry, flooring, and fixtures) and site constraints (narrow side yards, hillside conditions, or limited access) can materially affect overall cost. California’s evolving ADU regulations have reduced some cost elements by limiting impact fees for smaller ADUs and standardizing certain approval timelines, but local fees and inspections still add to budgets. For homeowners evaluating financial feasibility, state and industry sources recommend building detailed line-item budgets that include contingency allowances for unforeseen conditions, particularly on older Los Angeles housing stock where utility and structural upgrades are common.
