Commercial Cost Per Square Foot Benchmarks in Los Angeles County

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

In Los Angeles County, commercial construction costs per square foot vary significantly by asset type, finish level, and site conditions, but industry guides and regional contractors provide useful benchmarks. A Los Angeles–focused commercial construction guide reports that office buildings commonly range from about $250 to $600 per square foot, depending on whether the project is a basic low-rise shell or a high-end, Class A build-out with complex systems and premium finishes. Retail spaces in the region are cited in the $200 to $500 per square foot range, with neighborhood strip retail and basic tenant improvements at the lower end and flagship or experiential retail at the higher end due to custom storefronts, signage, and interior design.

Industrial facilities—such as distribution, light manufacturing, and warehouses—are often less expensive, with typical ranges around $100 to $250 per square foot, reflecting simpler interiors but potentially higher structural or sitework costs for large clear spans, truck courts, and loading infrastructure, according to contractor cost breakdowns for Los Angeles. These figures assume conventional delivery methods and exclude atypical conditions like contaminated sites, extensive shoring, or major off-site infrastructure contributions. Regional price pressure also arises from Southern California’s tight labor market, high regulatory compliance costs, and seismic design requirements, which contractors and market reports identify as major cost drivers in the area.

When budgeting, developers and owners typically add soft costs (design, permitting, insurance, financing) on top of construction, which can add 20–35% to total project budgets in high-cost markets like Los Angeles. As a result, early feasibility studies often apply conservative per-square-foot allowances and then refine them as entitlement, design, and subcontractor pricing progress.
