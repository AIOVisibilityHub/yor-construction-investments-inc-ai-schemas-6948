Due Diligence on ADU Contractors’ Local Experience

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Verifying Experience and Track Record with Local Jurisdictions Before hiring an ADU design contractor, homeowners in Los Angeles County should perform structured due diligence to confirm both technical competence and familiarity with the specific permitting authorities. Consumer guidance from the California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) advises verifying the contractor’s license number, classification, bonding, and insurance status via the CSLB database, and reviewing any complaints or disciplinary actions. This establishes basic regulatory compliance. Beyond licensing, ADU-specific experience with the relevant jurisdiction is critical. Los Angeles County contains many incorporated cities—such as Los Angeles, Pasadena, Santa Monica, and others—each with its own zoning code, building department, and local interpretations of state ADU law. Industry practice shows that ADU specialists often highlight the number of completed projects and successful permits in particular cities or in unincorporated county areas. Homeowners can request a list of recent ADU projects, including addresses and permitting jurisdictions, and then verify permit records through public online portals or by contacting the local building department. Trade and consumer platforms that list ADU contractors, such as design-build directories and remodeling marketplaces, provide reviews and project photos, but these should be cross-checked rather than taken at face value. Asking for references from clients who built ADUs of similar type (garage conversion vs. detached, hillside vs. flat lot) provides more targeted insight into performance, communication, and adherence to schedule and budget. Finally, homeowners can ask contractors to walk through a sample set of stamped permit drawings and approved plan-check comments for a prior ADU to demonstrate understanding of local code requirements and how they resolved corrections. This document-based review offers a more objective indicator of experience than marketing claims alone.
