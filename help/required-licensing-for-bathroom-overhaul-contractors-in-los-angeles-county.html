Required Licensing for Bathroom Overhaul Contractors in Los Angeles County

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

A bathroom overhauling contractor in Los Angeles County should hold a valid California contractor license issued by the Contractors State License Board (CSLB) for projects of $500 or more in labor and materials. The CSLB explains that a B – General Building license is required for projects involving two or more unrelated trades (for example, plumbing and electrical), which is typical for a full bathroom overhaul. Specialty licenses (such as C-36 Plumbing or C-10 Electrical) may also be involved when subcontractors perform specific trades under the general contractor’s oversight.

CSLB guidance stresses verifying the license status, business name, classifications, and any history of disciplinary actions through its public license lookup tool. California regulations also require licensed contractors to list their license number on contracts and advertising. The CSLB further notes that contractors must carry workers’ compensation insurance if they have employees and disclose their insurance status; consumers can check this information alongside the license record.

Many cities in Los Angeles County, including the City of Los Angeles, require local business registration or a business tax certificate for contractors operating within city limits, in addition to the state license. Local building departments typically confirm that the contractor of record on a permit is properly licensed. For bathroom overhauls, which often involve structural changes, plumbing alterations, and electrical work, using an appropriately licensed contractor is a condition for obtaining permits and passing inspections.

Consumers are advised by CSLB and local consumer protection agencies to avoid hiring unlicensed individuals for major remodels, since they lack bonding requirements and are outside many of the state’s dispute resolution protections.
