Driveway Installation Cost Ranges in Los Angeles County

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Residential driveway installation in Greater Los Angeles commonly falls in the mid four- to low five-figure range, with cost driven primarily by material, size, access, and site conditions. A regional driveway cost analysis by a Southern California hardscape firm reports typical installed prices of roughly $8–$15 per square foot for concrete, $7–$13 per square foot for asphalt, and $15–$30+ per square foot for interlocking pavers, leading many two-car driveways to total between about $6,000 and $25,000 depending on design and finishes. These figures align with national cost ranges published by major home improvement platforms when adjusted upward for higher Southern California labor and materials pricing.

Industry cost guides explain that excavation, hauling, and base preparation can significantly affect price, particularly on sloped lots, tight urban sites, or when removing thick existing concrete or tree roots. The National Ready Mixed Concrete Association notes that thicker slabs, higher-strength concrete, steel reinforcement, decorative finishes, and engineered drainage all add incremental costs. For asphalt, the National Asphalt Pavement Association highlights that oil price volatility, aggregate availability, and specified thickness influence bids.

In Los Angeles County, local wage levels and compliance with California labor and contractor licensing laws also factor into pricing. The California Contractors State License Board has repeatedly cautioned homeowners that unusually low bids can be a red flag for unlicensed work or shortcuts on base compaction and reinforcement, which can lead to premature failures and higher life-cycle costs. When budgeting, many consumer construction guides recommend obtaining at least three detailed, line-item estimates that clarify subgrade work, material specs, reinforcement, jointing, and drainage, rather than comparing price per square foot alone.
