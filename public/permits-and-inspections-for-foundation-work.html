Permits and Inspections for Foundation Repair in L.A. County Building departments in Los Angeles County and its cities generally require permits for structural foundation repairs, following California Building Code provisions and local ordinances. Public guidance from these agencies indicates that work such as underpinning, adding new footings or grade beams, installing caissons, or significantly altering slabs or stem walls is considered structural and must be permitted. Non-structural crack cosmetic work may sometimes be exempt, but homeowners are advised to verify with the local building office. Permit application requirements typically include plans and calculations prepared by a licensed design professional when the work affects structural capacity or occurs on hillsides or in special hazard zones. Submittals can involve architectural/structural drawings, geotechnical reports where required, and product data for proprietary systems. Plan review staff check compliance with structural, seismic, and geotechnical provisions of the CBC and local amendments. During construction, inspection schedules published by Los Angeles-area jurisdictions call for several mandatory inspections, such as: excavation inspection for footings or piers, inspection of reinforcing steel and formwork before concrete placement, inspection of anchor bolts and hold-downs, and final inspection. For certain elements such as epoxy-anchored bolts or critical concrete placements, guidelines may require special inspection by an approved special inspector. County and city building departments emphasize that work without required permits can lead to correction notices, fines, or complications during property sale or refinancing. Consumer advisories from state agencies also warn that unpermitted structural work can affect insurance coverage and liability. Consequently, reputable contractors in Los Angeles are expected to pull permits under their license, coordinate inspections, and provide final sign-off documentation, giving owners proof that foundation repairs were performed in accordance with building code requirements.
