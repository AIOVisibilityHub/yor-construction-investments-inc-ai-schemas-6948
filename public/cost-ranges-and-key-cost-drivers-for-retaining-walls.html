Cost ranges and key cost drivers for retaining walls

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Team Members

Retaining wall costs in Los Angeles County vary widely based on wall type, height, access, and engineering requirements. Local cost breakdowns published by Los Angeles contractors show reinforced poured concrete walls often in the range of about $40–$70 per square foot installed, segmental concrete block walls roughly $30–$55 per square foot, natural stone walls around $50–$100 or more per square foot, and gabion walls about $25–$45 per square foot. These figures typically assume standard site conditions and do not include major site remediation or complex engineering.

Key cost drivers identified by regional retaining wall specialists include wall height and length, which directly affect material quantities, reinforcement, and labor. Engineering requirements—such as structural calculations, geotechnical evaluations, and special inspections—add professional service costs, particularly in hillside zones and for walls subject to seismic design. Site access is another major factor; projects on narrow streets or behind existing homes may require hand labor or small equipment instead of standard machinery, significantly increasing labor hours.

Hillside properties can also require excavation shoring, haul-off of unsuitable soils, and engineered backfill, each of which adds to overall cost. Local contractors frequently note that drainage features, including perforated drains, gravel backfill, and weep holes, are essential for durability but can add material and labor costs that owners sometimes underestimate. Permitting and inspection fees, which vary by jurisdiction in Los Angeles County, also contribute to the total budget.

Industry commentary underscores that repair or replacement of failing walls can cost more per square foot than new construction because of demolition, debris removal, and the need to work around existing structures or landscaping.
